About the project

See how we designed My Notes, an AI-native notes app with a calm, distraction-free workspace that captures ideas, automates routines, and keeps focus.

Note-taking sits at an unusual intersection right now. Users have access to more capture tools than any previous generation, and yet cognitive load has never been higher. Every meeting produces an audio file that could be transcribed. Every idea competes with three notifications for attention. The result is a category where AI could theoretically solve the friction and where most tools instead layer more features on top of an already crowded surface. My Notes was designed against that pattern from the first sprint.

The design work centered on capture that stays out of the user's way and organization that happens without deliberate effort. Audio recordings, text notes, and quick captures all flow into the same workspace, where the AI does the sorting, tagging, and summarization the user would otherwise have to do manually. That workflow shift is what earns the case its place in the summary AI note take conversation, because most competitors treat AI summarization as a feature buried under three menu levels rather than the operating logic of the entire interface.

Design Language

A Calm, Distraction-Free Surface

  • The visual system rests on three ideas. Frosted glass softens every panel. Dynamic gradients shift warmth across the interface. A mix of clean sans-serif and dot-matrix type adds texture without noise. Together they create a space that feels premium and quiet. Nothing competes for attention. The interface recedes so the writing can come forward.

Typography

Two Voices, One System

  • Urbanist carries the interface. It is open, geometric, and easy to read at any size. Headings run at 64px Medium with tight tracking and generous line height, giving titles room to breathe. A second face, Matricha, handles the small dot-matrix labels and timestamps. It nods to the language of screens and signals without ever crowding the page. The pairing keeps the product feeling modern and human at once.

Colors

Light, Gradient, Air

  • The palette is built for softness. Soft Cyan (#93DCDF), Warm Peach (#EFAF86), and Lavender (#BB96DA) blend into the fluid gradients that define every surface. Pure Black (#000000) and White (#FFFFFF) anchor the system. They hold contrast for text and controls while the pastels do the emotional work. The result feels less like a tool and more like daylight.

Mobile App

Welcome to a Living Workspace

  • The app opens on "Your notes," a glass-like board that adapts to how each person thinks. Notes, checklists, files, and voice memos share one calm surface. There are no rigid folders to fight against. A built-in information architecture ux keeps everything reachable in a tap, from a quick to-do to a full lecture transcript. Collaboration happens in place, with shared notes and live editing between teammates.

Key Features

Key Features

      The modular system lifts your most active tasks to the surface. Manage a to-do list straight from the dashboard and save mental energy.

    • Always in Context

    • Fluid Editing

      A dynamic action layer attaches to whatever you select. Format and enrich text at the speed of thought while the interface stays clean.

    Fluid Thoughts

Three pillars carry the story. The Fluid Workspace adapts to your flow. Native Intelligence acts as a co-pilot that generates and summarizes. Seamless Sync captures voice memos and keeps collaboration instant. The page reads like the product feels: calm, confident, and quietly powerful.

    Brand & Social

    Pushing Boundaries

    • The brand lives on a radiant starburst mark and the gradient system that runs through the whole product. Across social channels the message stays consistent. No noise. Just focus. Each post treats fluid form and AI as the headline, framing My Notes as a workspace where ideas breathe and crystallize. The @mynotes profile carries the same line and has grown a following of nearly 13K. Every touchpoint says the same thing in a different way.

    Impact

    Built for Focus

    • By uniting the interface, the AI, and the brand into one fluid system, My Notes turned a cluttered category into something that feels like relief. Consistency across every surface builds trust, and an AI-native core gives the product a real foothold in the digital transformation of how people think and write.

    Key Results
